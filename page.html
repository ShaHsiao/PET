Research Problems

PET studies how an AI pet can help people set meaningful goals, stay motivated, and turn intentions into sustainable actions.

Problem 01

Maintaining Motivation

How can a virtual companion help a person remain engaged with a long-term goal?

Problem 02

Meaningful Personalization

How should PET adapt its personality, feedback, and support to each person?

Problem 03

From Goals to Action

How can PET help translate a broad goal into practical, achievable daily steps?

Research Issues

Building a persuasive AI companion also creates important technical, ethical, and social questions.

Privacy & Data

PET may learn from sensitive personal information, so users need transparency and control.

Trust & Safety

Advice must be responsible, understandable, and appropriate to PET's role and limitations.

Evaluation

We need reliable ways to measure engagement, behavior change, well-being, and long-term effects.

About

Personalized gEnerative Tamagotchi (PET) is an AI pet designed to support people as they pursue self-directed goals. PET explores how personalization, companionship, and generative AI can make behavior interventions more engaging and supportive.

This website documents the motivation, research questions, development process, and outcomes of the project.
